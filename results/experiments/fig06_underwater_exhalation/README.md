# Fig. 6 — Underwater exhalation

A rendered frame of the exhalation scene.

**Not reproducible from this repository.** The image is an offline render, and
the renderer is not driven from here. Only the finished frame ships.

What does ship is the scene itself, in `dataset/exhalation/`: the tracked bubble
files carrying the BEM, learned and Minnaert frequency columns. Those are what
the paper's numbers for this scene are computed from.

The scene's timing row is in
[`table01_timing_across_scenes/`](../table01_timing_across_scenes), and its
end-to-end cost breakdown in
[`fig08_end_to_end_timing/`](../fig08_end_to_end_timing).
